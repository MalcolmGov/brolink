Talk track — open

Admin is the product. If we take chasing, status, FNOL reading, phone wrap, and first-draft endorsements off the broker and off the floor — with traces the insurer can supervise — Brolink becomes the platform of the broker of tomorrow. We sit on Websure. We do not rebuild it.

Do not say
  • Replace Websure, or “Websure AI v2.4”
  • Fully autonomous settlement or underwriting — including auto-binding a young driver
  • Headcount takeout (People is pillar 1)
  • Santam-only examples as the whole story
  • Pricing, partnership, or “pilot” in this hour
Questions to leave with
  • Which mailbox is the live broker desk, and who owns it?
  • What does Websure already expose — API, webhooks, or UI only?
  • Which insurer book would be in scope first, and how is Santam data separated from Hollard today?
  • Where is the backlog worst: email, the desk DID, FNOL docs, or endorsements?
  • Do you already record the broker-desk DID, and who owns that recording?
  • Is there a Santam group AI / vendor policy, and who clears it?
  • Do you want this as a capability you own, or one we operate?
If they say yes — 90-day discovery (paid)
